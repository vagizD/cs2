06 / Macro Synthesis

The 90-Day CS2 Roster Lifecycle: Empirical Conclusions

Macroeconomic findings across 39 isolated 1-player substitutions • 1,107 Tier 1 matches • min. 2 matches/window
RQ1 Answered & Validated
Click image to inspect high-res
Stage I: The Pre-Change Slump (Weeks -4 to 0)

Form drops sharply to a 35% win rate trough at Week -2. Impending benchings destroy trust, tactical cohesion, and motivation.

Stage II: The Adaptation Valley (Weeks 1 to 4)

Initial disruption shock: 38% WR at Week 1. Role overlap, playbook adjustments, and comms gaps produce short-term drag.

Stage III: The Synergy Peak (Weeks 5 to 9)

Rapid monotonic ascent reaching 55% win rate at Week 8 (50–60 days). The playbook is internalized and role clarity is restored.

Definitive Empirical Answer to RQ1
  • Does performance improve? Yes (+19% win rate gain from trough to peak).
  • Is it sustained? In 50%–51% of cases, teams maintain a positive long-term trajectory over the 90-day window without further benchings.

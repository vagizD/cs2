Workflow: From Raw Web to Insights

Six structured stages from scraping to empirical validation.

2
Collect Web Pages

3
SQLite Relational Database
Clean structured storage linking tournaments, matches, maps, and weekly 5-player rosters.
4
Infer Roster Changes
Algorithmic detection of exact 1-player weekly additions and removals in ranking snapshots.

Aggregate Statistics per Move

12 / Limitations

Study Limitations

Interpreting findings with scientific rigor, statistical humility, and real-world esports context.

📉
Overlapping CIs
Core 4 and team 95% confidence intervals overlap by ≥ 60% — indicating strong directional signals rather than definitive mathematical proof.
💎
High-Quality Data Points, But Rare
Strict Tier 1 isolation filters out noisy disbandments, isolating 39 pristine cases — prioritizing clean-room analytical purity over sheer sample size.
🚪
Player Agency & Internal Friction
Not all moves are performance cuts: players may retire, take breaks, seek transfers, or experience friction with teammates, coaches, or organizational politics.
